# Reader's Motivation

Count the days, and the urges you beat. For something to stop (sugar, cigarettes, alcohol,
screens in the evening) or something to do every day. One of the
[Reader's](https://github.com/funkypitt/readers-launcher) apps: text only, white on black,
six languages, nothing leaves the phone (no network permission).

## Key points

* **One page per habit**: "without sugar since 1 October", the day in big letters, the urges
  beaten, what you avoided in units and money if you gave a count, the next step ("day 2 of one
  week", then "5 days to go before 66 days"), your own reason, and the last five weeks as a
  row of squares.
* **A slip costs one day and nothing else.** On the day of a slip the headline is what stays
  earned ("19 days held", out of 20, best run 19 days), not "day 0"; the totals and the urges
  beaten never go down; the app says "a slip, not a relapse", gives your plan back, and starts
  again now.
* **"An urge, right now"**: three minutes with a breathing ring (3 s in, 3 s hold, 6 s out),
  a countdown, your reason and your plan. Then "it passed" counts an urge beaten, or "I slipped".
* **"I held out"**: one tap counts an urge beaten, from the page or from the home-screen
  widget's ✓.
* **A plan decided in advance**: "when the urge comes, I…", asked when the habit is created,
  shown when the urge comes and after a slip. A start date that marks a fresh start: today, next
  Monday, the first of the month.
* **Tell someone where you are**: one line to share (the day, the count), because a count said
  to someone holds better.
* A record of every entry (a wrong tap can be removed), a daily reminder at the hour you
  choose (off unless you turn it on), a backup saved then shared, a widget for any launcher.

Why it is built this way, with the studies behind each choice: [docs/revue.md](docs/revue.md)
(in French).

## Install

- **F-Droid** (recommended, updates arrive by themselves): add the repository from [gallaz.ch/apps](https://gallaz.ch/apps/#fdroid), or the address `https://funkypitt.github.io/fdroid-repo/repo` in F-Droid.
- **Obtainium**: add `https://github.com/funkypitt/readers-motivation` in Obtainium.
- **APK**: attached to the [latest release](../../releases/latest). No automatic updates.

All three deliver the same file, with the same signature.

## Build

```
export JAVA_HOME=/path/to/jdk-21
./gradlew assembleDebug
```

minSdk 28, targetSdk 34. The six `strings.xml` are generated by `python3 tools/strings.py`.
